## Image contract

Published JPGs are byte-identical copies. WebP derivatives use scripts/optimize_images.py process(), only for the new assets: widths up to 960 and 1920, no upscale, aspect ratio preserved, ICC profile carried through. No crop, retouching, colour adjustment or artwork changes. The viewer automatically loads the original when zoom exceeds derivative resolution.
