# Desktop/SP import curation

Reviewed all 100 raster files under `~/Desktop/SP` (88 PNG and 12 JPEG); the folder contained no PDF, AI, PSD, or TIFF files. Source files were read only. Selected files were copied byte-for-byte into the portfolio originals; no source artwork was cropped, recolored, or retouched. WebP derivatives use the existing resize/compression pipeline. The 12 JPEGs in SP carried sRGB profiles; PNG sources were RGBA without embedded profiles. No CMYK source was found.

## Published selections

- **MATCHA TIMES (12):** signature campaign, product lineup, menu, taiyaki collaboration, Asakusa flyer (front/back), Sakura Stand, ceremonial matcha campaign, cocktails menu, Uji latte, Happy Hour, Toyosu store sign.
- **Machida Shoten (9):** Garlic Monster, lunch menu, spicy ramen, Sorya voucher (front/back), soda floats, Japanese sweets, All Ramen discount, Phnom Penh brand promotion.
- **Origin Hunter Omakase (9):** tuna editorial sequence, uni and crab features, seafood illustration, omakase menu.
- **Golden Chicken (2):** horizontal wordmark and roundel.
- **JFB Presents (2):** drink menu and origin sake selection.
- **Hakkaisan (2):** Hakkaisan promotion and Mokkiri sake promotion.
- **Wagokoro Pizza (1):** pocket pizza promotion, separated from the mixed-client Machida folder.
- **Existing pending projects published:** Ootoya (1), CIMA (2), Volante (2).

Year and region metadata remain unset where the artwork did not establish them. The unrelated pending projects remain unchanged.

## Exclusions and duplicates

No byte-identical or decoded-pixel-identical files were found. CIMA and Volante each included two additional translated front/back sets using the same layout; the English pair was selected for each. Matcha Times campaign alternatives and carousel slides were reduced to representative work. Origin Hunter pages were curated to nine examples. A Machida folder artwork combining several restaurant brands and a multi-client coupon sheet were excluded; Wagokoro Pizza was kept as its own client.
